FAQ

Questions buyers often ask

Start with the basics, then send us your exact requirements for a tailored quotation.

Can I request OEM / private label customization?

Yes. Logo, packaging and selected product customization can be discussed based on the model and order requirements.

What should I include in a quotation request?

Product/model, estimated quantity, destination market, target price if available, logo or packaging requirements and preferred delivery timing.

Can I request samples before placing an order?

Sample availability depends on the product. Mention your required models in the inquiry and the sales team can confirm options.

Do you support mixed-product sourcing?

You can send multiple models or categories in one request. We can organize the inquiry and confirm availability, MOQ and pricing.
